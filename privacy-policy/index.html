Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and protect your information.

Last updated: 13 November 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide when you book an appointment, fill an enquiry form, call or message us. This may include your name, phone number, email address, age, gender, preferred service and any health-related details you choose to share. We also collect limited technical data such as browser type, device and pages visited through cookies and analytics.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to schedule and confirm appointments, respond to enquiries, provide patient care, send important service messages, improve our website and meet legal obligations. We do not sell your personal information.

3. Cookies and Analytics

Our website uses cookies and similar technologies to remember preferences and understand how visitors use the site. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, although some features may not work as expected.

4. Sharing of Information

We share information only with doctors and staff involved in your care, with service providers who help operate our systems under confidentiality obligations, with insurers where you request cashless services, and with authorities where required by law.

5. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical and physical safeguards to protect your information against unauthorised access, loss or misuse. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Patient Rights

You may request access to the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct inaccurate information, and withdraw your consent for non-essential communication at any time by contacting us using the details below.

7. Data Retention

We keep personal information only as long as necessary for the purposes described here, for medical record requirements and to comply with applicable laws.

8. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party sites such as Google Maps and social media. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those websites.

9. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page with the revised date.
